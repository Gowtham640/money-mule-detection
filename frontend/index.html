How Early Warning Works

Each transaction updates behavioural signals for the sender and receiver (velocity, short gaps, high value, fan-in/out, repeated amounts, dormant activation, shared device, risky neighbours). Signals stack into a warning score that decays every few seconds.

An account turns yellow when its score is above the adaptive threshold (the smoothed 90th percentile of active accounts, clamped to 0.40–0.68) and at least two signals are active.
